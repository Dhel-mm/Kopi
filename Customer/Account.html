Kopi; Calamba

Established on February 9, 2021, Kopi; Calamba is your cozy neighborhood café in the heart of Lingga, Calamba. We're open every Tuesday to Sunday, 4:00 PM to 11:00 PM. Visit us at 72 Villanueva Subdivision, Camia Street, Barangay Lingga, Calamba, Laguna.

Find Us Online:

Kopi; Calamba Main

Whether you're looking for your favorite coffee, a refreshing non-coffee drink, a creamy frappe, a soothing tea, or a fruity soda, we have something to satisfy every craving. Pair your favorite drink with our delicious rice meals, chicken meals, and tasty snacks while enjoying our cozy ambiance. Come, relax, enjoy, and discover your new favorite treats at Kopi; Calamba!
