Stock counts full and partly used packages as whole numbers. A package remains counted until empty. Measurement shows the total amount left. Set the full package size in Ingredient unit conversion.

Low stock parameters. An ingredient becomes Low Stock when its available amount reaches its reorder level. The reorder level uses four parameters: (1) the package baseline — 25% of one package, or one package for Sugar, Condensed Milk and Sprite; (2) average daily usage from orders in the last 14 days; (3) lead time — days a restock takes to arrive; and (4) safety stock days. Reorder level = the higher of the package baseline and average daily usage × (lead time + safety days). You can also save a manual reorder level per ingredient with the button. Zero is Out of Stock.
