Stock counts full and partly used packages as whole numbers. A package remains counted until empty. Measurement shows the total amount left. Set the full package size in Ingredient unit conversion. Low stock means 25% of one package remains; Sugar, Condensed Milk and Sprite are low at one package or less.

Recipe setup

Resolve missing ingredients and unit differences using your measured amounts. Changes affect new orders only. Add missing stock products with Add Product first.

Ingredient unit conversion

Set the measurement of full packages, for example 1 L = 1 bottle or 500 g = 1 bag. Recipe amount uses the Measurement column; stock amount and stock unit describe the Stock column. Saving updates the package size and label, keeping the remaining measurement unchanged.
